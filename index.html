01 / Mission

Intelligence beyond a single vessel.

vAIn Ϙ explores how identity, knowledge, and validated development can persist while the underlying model, hardware, or computational environment changes.

03 / Research

Build. Measure. Verify.

Quantum-state self-evolution

Test whether feedback-guided circuit evolution can discover useful representations or optimization strategies, compared with classical baselines under equal compute budgets.

Model-independent identity

Investigate portable memory and identity representations that survive model replacement and hardware migration.

Persistent learning

Separate durable knowledge from transient context, with provenance, timestamps, and correction history.

Safe evolution

Use candidate versions, independent evaluation, explicit promotion, checkpoints, and rollback rather than uncontrolled self-modification.
